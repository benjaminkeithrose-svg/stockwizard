# 0DTE experiment matrix, modelled prices (2026-11-17)

> **How to read this (added after the run):** the survivors mostly measure the model's own assumption. Prices come from the VIX/VIX1D, which normally sit above the moves that actually happen, so selling premium every day profits in the model almost by construction. Real bid/ask spreads, skew and fast-day fills, which decide the real answer, aren't in the model. QQQ is priced at a guessed 1.2 × the VIX, and its many survivors show how much results depend on such guesses. The validation period was fairly calm. None of the survivors fits the $50 limit. **This shows the framework works; it is not evidence of profit.** The final untouched period hasn't been looked at, and stays a fair test for real-quote runs.

- **Modelled option prices, not evidence.** Black-Scholes with put skew and an estimated bid-ask spread; real quotes are needed before any of this means anything (docs/research/0DTE-RESEARCH.md).
- Free hourly candles: entries only on the half hour (10:30 stands in for the brief's 10:00), and stops checked at each hour's high and low.
- XSP and SPX are modelled from SPY's price; QQQ is priced with 1.2 × the VIX. Fees and spreads per instrument are estimates.
- Inflation and jobs-report days aren't tagged yet (their dates need checking); Fed days and expiration Fridays are.

Periods: development 2024-11-19 to 2025-08-29; validation to 2026-03-31; final untouched to 2026-11-16 (not looked at).

**Tried 270 strategies** (260 with at least 20 development trades). False-discovery check at q = 0.1: **74 survived**.
